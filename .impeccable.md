## Design Context

### Users
Students of system design, data structures, algorithms, and LeetCode. They open this IDE when a theory or problem does not click, work through a guide, mini game, or step-by-step session, then prompt Cursor (via the harness) to generate the next artifact in the same app.

### Brand Personality
Focused, instructional, utilitarian. Calm desk energy: paper, ink, a teal mark — not a dashboard, not a marketing site. Confidence from clarity, not decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light paper field (`#e8e4dc` / `#f4f1ea`), IBM Plex Sans + Mono, sharp 2px corners, teal accent used sparingly. Anti-references: neon-on-dark “AI” chrome, equal-weight card columns, shouting sidebars, identical icon+heading+text grids as the whole page.

### Design Principles
1. **The artifact is the product.** Resource tiles, guide bodies, games, and step-by-step sessions are primary. Taxonomy, filters, and chrome are tools.
2. **Progressive disclosure.** Secondary navigation stays closed until asked for. Do not park a browse column on every list tab.
3. **One primary surface per page.** Squint test: the heading and the work (tiles or lesson) win. Controls sit on the same rank as each other, below that.
4. **Quiet secondary.** Browse and Filter are text-level actions, not boxed destinations. Ghost/text over bordered primary.
5. **Say it once.** No hint copy that restates a control’s name. Breadcrumbs on tiles already show category / subcategory.
